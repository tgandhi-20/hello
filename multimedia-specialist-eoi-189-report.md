## ⚠️ Data-access note

The official SkillSelect EOI dashboard
(`https://api.dynamic.reports.employment.gov.au/anonap/extensions/hSKLS02_SkillSelect_EOI_Data/hSKLS02_SkillSelect_EOI_Data.html`)
could **not** be reached from this environment: the session's network egress policy denies
all Australian government hosts (proxy CONNECT → 403 policy denial), and the sites also
reject server-side fetchers. The figures below were assembled from web-search results that
republish the official Department of Home Affairs SkillSelect data, and could not be
verified against the dashboard directly. Treat them as indicative until confirmed on the
official dashboard.

## Subclass 189 invitation rounds — 2025–26 program year

Rounds now run roughly quarterly. Three rounds were held in the 2025–26 program year:

| Round date | Invitations issued (189) | Minimum points range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 Aug 2025 | ~6,887 | 65–110 | First round of 2025–26; trades from 65 pts, ICT/professional roles 90–95+ |
| 13 Nov 2025 | 10,000 | 65+ | 65 pts applied to priority trades (carpenters, bricklayers etc.); professional/ICT roles ~90+ |
| 4 Jun 2026 | 10,000 | 65–100 | Tie-break date 24 Apr 2026; trades from 65, nurses/teachers/social workers ~75, top specialist occupations up to 100 |

Next 189 round is expected **by 30 September 2026**.

## Multimedia Specialist (261211) specifics

- One aggregated source reported 261211 receiving invitations at **100 points** in the
  4 June 2026 round; other round summaries state that **ICT specialist occupations were
  not invited** in that round. These reports conflict, and the occupation-level result
  for 261211 could not be verified against the official dashboard from this environment.
- Multiple analyses agree that for general professional/ICT occupations (which includes
  2612), effective 189 cut-offs in 2025–26 sat around **90–95+ points**, i.e. realistic
  only with superior English, a skilled partner, or similar point boosters.

## EOI pool context (as of June 2026)

- **178,652** submitted EOIs were awaiting invitation in the subclass 189 pool, across
  **204 occupations** (all occupations, not just 2612).
- Occupation-level EOI counts by points score and status (SUBMITTED / INVITED / HOLD)
  for 261211 are published only on the official Qlik dashboard and were not retrievable
  from this environment.

## How to get the full filtered dashboard data

To capture the complete 261211 × 189 dataset (EOI counts by points score, EOI status,
and month) the official dashboard must be reachable. Options:

1. **Adjust this environment's network policy** (Claude Code on the web → environment
   settings → network access) to allow `api.dynamic.reports.employment.gov.au`, then the
   in-session browser can drive the dashboard filters and capture every table.
2. Open the dashboard manually: filter *Occupation* → 2612/261211, *Visa type* →
   189 Skilled Independent, then read counts by *Points score* and *EOI status*.
3. Third-party mirrors that republish the dashboard monthly: immitrend.com.au,
   eoidata.com, anzscosearch.com/eoi-backlog.
